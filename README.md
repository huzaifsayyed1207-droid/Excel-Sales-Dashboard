# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel Sales Performance Dashboard created using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Dynamic Slicers** to analyze sales revenue, profit margins, product category performance, and regional sales distribution.

![Sales Performance Dashboard](images/dashboard-preview.png)

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Highlights

- **Dynamic Slicers:** Connected to all 5 Pivot Tables and Charts to filter data seamlessly by `Region` and `Item Category`.
- **Regional Performance Analysis:** Regional sales and profit comparison across East, West, North, and South.
- **Product Category Breakdown:** Category-wise revenue and volume distribution (Chairs, Notebooks, USB Drives, etc.).
- **Top Customer Analysis:** Identification of high-value customers contributing to overall revenue and profit.
- **Sales Tier Matrix:** Sales classification across High, Medium, and Low tiers per region.
- **Monthly Trend Analysis:** Time-series line chart tracking monthly sales and profit movement throughout the year.
